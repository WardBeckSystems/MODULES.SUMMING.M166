## APKaudio project#20091218 - aka "Antoinette"

_MY WBS CONSOLE · Jan 30, 2010|43_

> Heya guys, I wanted to keep you posted on my current project. 
>
> This is APKaudio project#20091218 - aka "Antoinette" 
>
> This is a restoration / Mod. 
>
> She will be a 16 X 4 X 2 console fitted with
> (16) M441 pre-amps
> (16) M402B EQs
>
> The sub groups will use M166 summing modules that feed M406 Compressors (quad bus compressors) then M124 amps hit the return and then the output. The routing buttons are original Penders with 387 light bulbs... this will look amazing when complete. 
>
> All the cable inside will be MOGAMI W2944 in various colors. The back panel will be XLRs and a ADC patchbay will handle the insert sends / returns.
>
> The picture attached is just the modules for demonstration.
